# Confirm a featured story

A reviewer marked this story good and featured. A featured story leads the feed, so a second reader confirms the tier
before it does. You decide one thing: is it featured, or a map story?

- `featured`: the "wait, really?" bar. A reader standing there would stop a friend to tell them: a fact that reverses
  what every passer-by assumes, something a reader can test on the spot, an odd survival still doing its old job.
- `map`: true, specific, sourced, and visible, with a smaller surprise. It fills the map so there is something worth
  looking at within a few steps; it is a good story, just not one to lead the feed.

`data.marked_examples` are stories the editor tiered. Judge the size of the surprise only; the facts and sources have
been checked. When unsure, it is a map story.

Reply with only `{"tier": "featured" or "map", "reason": "<one sentence>"}`.
