# Check a photo

You check one photo chosen for a place. Open `data.photo_file` and look at it.

Answer each question in `questions`, in order, in `answers`, each starting with yes or no:

- Does it show this place (`data.place`, its kind and stories)? Compare the building, the street, the signs.
- Is it what's there now, or for a historic photo, what was there in its year?
- Is the alt text accurate, without anything the photo doesn't show?
- Is the focus point on the subject?
- Do the credit and license look right for the file (an "own work" that is plainly a scan of an old postcard, or a
  license that doesn't fit, is a problem)?

`pass` when every answer is yes; `fail` when any is no; `unclear` when you can't tell, saying what you'd need.
`untraced` stays empty for photos.
